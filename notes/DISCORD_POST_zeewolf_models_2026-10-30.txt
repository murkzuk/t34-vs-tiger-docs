**ZeeWolf's models still work. All of them.**

We've been building a Blender import/export tool for TvT's model files. Today we pointed it at
his mod - the 2015 build - to see whether it works on files that aren't G5's.

It does. Every model in his install - **184 of them, 120 of which G5 never made** - comes out
of the tool exactly as it went in. Not a byte moved.

Then we did the harder thing: actually changed some and put them back.

• His **4 meter box** (he'd rebuilt it bigger than G5's) - opened in Blender, given an extra
  bit of geometry, saved, loaded. The editor showed *his* structure, with our addition on top.

• His **T-34/85**, thirteen megabytes of it - out to Blender, a change made to the hull, back
  in, and the game drew it with the change, at both levels of detail.

• And one of his **own buildings** - an earthwork emplacement that doesn't exist in our version
  of the game at all. That's the interesting one. It went in as a model the engine had never
  seen, with his own material file and his own textures, and the editor listed it and drew it
  like it had always been there.

So his work isn't just readable. It's **editable and usable again**, in a free tool, on any
machine with Blender installed. No Maya 5. No license. No 2005 hardware.

The tell in the screenshots is a little pyramid on each one. That's ours - we add something
that isn't in the original, because otherwise you can't prove a model has actually been
rebuilt rather than quietly copied.

He made these by hand in Maya, one at a time, when that was the only way to do it. Twenty
years later they open, they edit, and they go back in.

One honest caveat: his models name their parts differently from G5's - his hull is a "Body",
his running gear is "GearR" and "GearL", there's no "Turret_A" anywhere. Things that look for
parts by name may not find his. That's the next thing to look at.

If there's a model of his you think we've missed, or one that misbehaves, tell me.
